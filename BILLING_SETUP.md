# PixiJoy AI — Play Console Billing Setup

Create these products in Google Play Console → Monetize → Products:

## Subscriptions
| Product ID | Type | Suggested price |
|------------|------|-----------------|
| `pixijoy_pro_monthly` | Subscription | ₹199/month |
| `pixijoy_pro_yearly` | Subscription | ₹1,499/year (add 7-day free trial) |

## One-time
| Product ID | Type | Suggested price |
|------------|------|-----------------|
| `pixijoy_pro_lifetime` | In-app product | ₹2,999 |

## Payment methods (automatic via Google Play)
- Google Pay
- PhonePe / UPI
- Credit & Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- PayPal (international regions)

## Testing
1. Add license testers in Play Console → Settings → License testing
2. Upload app to Internal testing track
3. Install from Play Store (not sideload) to test purchases
