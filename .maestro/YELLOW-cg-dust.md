# YELLOW / manual — classical dust trail

The CG sheet **Dust** button (`movie_cg_dusttrail`) adds a `DustTrail` plate. Motes drift along the layer yaw over the shot. Preview and export share `CgOverlays`. Positions are a pure function of index, yaw, and progress (unit-tested). This is classical canvas dust, not Filament particles and not neural. The chip stays **Live · classical CG**.

No device frame capture in this slice. Do not mark the trail green from the button tap alone.
