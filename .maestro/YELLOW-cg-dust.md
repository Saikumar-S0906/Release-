# YELLOW / manual — classical dust trail

The CG sheet **Dust** button (movie_cg_dusttrail) adds a DustTrail plate. Motes drift along the layer yaw over the shot and draw a short classical motion-blur crumb streak (shutter sample behind the head). Preview and export share CgOverlays. Positions are a pure function of index, yaw, and progress (unit-tested). This is classical canvas dust, not Filament particles and not neural. The chip stays **Live · classical CG**.

No device frame capture in this slice. Do not mark the trail green from the button tap alone.
